# TestFlight feedback, build 1.0 (4), 25–26 Nov 2026 — second round of fixes

Round 1 (in progress): voice (Lithuanian phrase, offline model download, five more languages),
quick actions as a distinct button with a grid, cart toast, Meals day strip and spacing, colour themes.

## Home and navigation
- Home: every card looks the same; no emphasis, unclear which part belongs where. Wants hierarchy,
  colour, icons, small charts, clear calls to action. "Grocery estimates" takes a lot of space.
- My Stock is hard to find (only on Home); reorganise buttons (My Stock, chat, quick actions).
- Reminders bell should live in settings, not on Home.
- Guided tour of the app (first run, and from Profile/help).

## Profile and settings
- Profile is one very long page: group settings into categories that open their own pages.
- Change name and profile picture.
- Daily targets (calories, macros) can't be changed: make them editable with "Use recommended".
- Workout reminder arrived although Workouts was turned off in onboarding: reminders of a turned-off
  feature must not be scheduled.
- Currency: tester in Canada asks for local currency and what prices are based on.

## Logging and stock
- Stock doesn't decrease when food is logged (only "Log meal from stock" and My Stock take from it).
- Show which stock items the app used for what I described (voice/photo/search review).
- Voice review card: show the quantity (e.g. 2 × veggie burger) next to grams and kcal.
- Snap a photo now and clarify later instead of describing every item.
- Upload a recipe I'm following (text or photo), calculate nutrition, ask servings, scale up/down.
- Calorie history and insights (charts over days/weeks) are hard to find or missing.
- Pieces per package: how many slices / samosas / cans an item has, so calories and weight divide
  per piece; log by pieces.
- Add-to-stock screen: "Serving" is the wrong word; it's the amount in the package. A barcode should
  fill the package size and serving size from Open Food Facts. Nutrition shown with long decimals
  (22.727273 g) and "slightly off": round and check per-100 g vs per-serving.
- Swipe "Remove" in My Stock should be red.

## Onboarding
- "Pick your foods": instead of picking every food, start from a suggested list based on the diet
  and goal that the person can edit (deselect, star). Hide or skip categories that conflict with the
  diet (no Dairy for vegans; soy milk etc. only where it fits).
- Favourite foods in onboarding: after picking foods within a category, offer "Select more" / Select all
  (the grouped Select all from Profile › Favourite foods should be used in onboarding too).
- Meals: an expanded meal card repeats itself (summary row with ingredients and kcal, then the same
  ingredients again in the expanded part): show the details once.
