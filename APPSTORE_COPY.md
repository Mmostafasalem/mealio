## Promotional text
Plan meals around what's in your kitchen, cook from 1,500 recipes, log food by
voice or search, shop to a weekly budget and train with guided workouts.

## Description
Nourlo brings meal planning, food logging, grocery budgeting and training into one app.

Track your day
Log foods and see estimated calories and macros against your personal targets.
Search about 13,600 foods, scan a barcode, or pick from today's plan in one tap.

Speak to log
Tap the microphone and say "two eggs and a coffee" or "add milk to my cart".
Speech is recognised on your iPhone, in English, German, Arabic or Lithuanian.

Plan your meals
Your week's meal plan is built on your phone from the food in your cart and
stock, split across the day the way you choose. Foods you don't have yet are
marked, and one tap adds them to your cart.

Cook with what you have
About 1,500 recipes from the Wikibooks Cookbook, matched to the food in your
stock, with nutrition per serving estimated from USDA data. Log what you cooked
in one tap. Works offline.

Organise your shop
Get a week of groceries fitted to your budget, built from the foods you like.
Build a cart and grocery list, set a budget and enter prices you find in-store.
Prices are estimates; they are not live shelf prices, promotions or guaranteed
availability.

Train
Build a workout for the gym, home, cardio or mobility, sized to your time and
level, with how-to drawings and motion demos, or build your own. Workout mode
times your rest and logs every set, weight and personal record, with progress
charts.

Works with Apple Health and widgets
Optionally save logged food and tracked workouts to Apple Health, and read your
weight, steps and active energy. Health data never leaves your device. Home
Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your calories and today's meals.

Chat with Nourlo
Ask Nourlo about meals, groceries and your targets, or for a new recipe. Online AI
is optional: continue as a free guest in Profile (no sign-up) and get 30 AI credits
a day; a chat message or an AI recipe costs 1. It only sees what you allow it to.

Your data stays yours
No account needed, no ads, no tracking. Optional private iCloud sync covers
shopping data; body measurements and food logs are not synced. Delete All Data
is in Profile.

Nourlo is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Nutrition
and calorie values are estimates. Check ingredients and allergens yourself and
consult a qualified professional about your individual dietary needs.

## Review notes
No login is needed: every feature except the optional AI chat works without an account.
Online AI (chat and AI recipes): Profile > AI allowance > Continue as a guest (free,
no sign-up, 30 credits a day), then turn on Profile > Privacy & Help > Online AI for
chat and recipes, then tap Nourlo on Home > Chat. Requests go through our backend to
Groq; Delete online account (Profile) or Delete All Data removes the account.
Meal plans, weekly groceries, workouts and the recipe library are built on the
device. Voice logging uses on-device speech recognition; the microphone and
speech permission are only asked when the microphone button is tapped.
Apple Health is off until turned on in Profile > Apple Health; it writes logged
food and tracked workouts and reads weight, steps and active energy. Health
data never leaves the device.
Optional iCloud sync (Profile) covers shopping data only.
Calorie and nutrition values are estimates, shown as such in the app; Nourlo
gives no medical advice. Grocery prices are estimates, not live retailer offers.
Third-party content and licences are in Profile > Acknowledgements (Wikibooks
recipes and exercise drawings CC BY-SA 4.0, motion capture CMU and CC BY 4.0,
offline speech model CC BY 4.0), credited where they are shown.
Support: nourlo.app.support@gmail.com
