# Acknowledgements

Nourlo is built on open data and open work. Thank you to everyone below.

## Food and nutrition

**USDA FoodData Central** (U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service). Nutrition for the offline food catalogue and for every recipe estimate. Public domain. https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/

**Open Food Facts** (packaged products, looked up online). Database under the Open Database License (ODbL); product content under the Database Contents License. https://world.openfoodfacts.org/

## Recipes

**Wikibooks Cookbook**, by its contributors. Recipes adapted by Nourlo: amounts read into grams and nutrition estimated from USDA data. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; the adapted recipes are shared under the same licence. Each recipe links to its original page. https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook:Recipes

## Exercise demos

**Workout Guide** by Bryl Lim, based on **Everkinetic** exercise illustrations. Adapted drawings (muscle areas added). Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. https://github.com/bryllim/workout-guide

**CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database** (mocap.cs.cmu.edu). Motion for the walking, running, squat and other clips. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

**ARKit vs Vicon exercise recordings** (Zenodo 6527283) and **100STYLE** (Ian Mason and colleagues). Motion clips. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

## Voice

**Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3** by NVIDIA, converted to Core ML by FluidInference: the optional offline speech model. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

**FluidAudio** by FluidInference: runs the offline speech model. Licensed Apache 2.0.
