# Mealio — Support

Need help with Mealio? We're happy to assist.

For help with this pre-release build, contact the person who provided it to you.
A verified public support contact and hosted support URL must be added before
App Store submission; no public mailbox is claimed by this document.

## FAQ

**Are grocery prices live?**
No. Prices are estimates from a curated catalog, not verified promotions,
live store inventory or guaranteed shelf prices. You can enter a real price in
the shopping flow. Check the retailer before purchasing.

**Why don't I see nearby stores?**
Make sure location access is enabled (Settings ▸ Mealio ▸ Location) and you have a
network connection. Without location, Mealio uses Vilnius centre.

**My meal plan didn't update after I changed my cart.**
You can tap **Regenerate** on the Meals tab. Online AI features require the
optional sharing permission in Profile and a working backend. Local suggestions
remain available when sharing is off or the service cannot be reached.

**Are the calorie numbers exact?**
No — calorie and macro values are **estimates** and vary by product and
preparation. Mealio is not a medical device.

**How do I reset my data?**
Profile ▸ **Delete All Data** clears local profile, log, cart, budget and plans.
If shopping sync was enabled, cloud deletion may remain pending until the app
reconnects with the original Apple Account. Health data on other devices stays
local to each device and must be deleted there.

**What does iCloud sync?**
Optional shopping sync covers cart, grocery list, budget and price corrections.
It does not sync body measurements, food logs, dietary restrictions or nutrition
plans. Use **Sync Now** or reopen the app to check for changes.

**Can I rely on suggestions for allergies?**
No. Preferences guide suggestions but cannot guarantee allergen safety. Check
every ingredient, product label and preparation method yourself.

**Does Mealio track me or sell my data?**
No. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
