# Mealio — Support

Need help with Mealio? We're happy to assist.

For technical issues in this pre-release build, use the
[Mealio issue tracker](https://github.com/Mmostafasalem/mealio/issues).
The tracker is public: do not post body measurements, food logs, account tokens,
API keys or other private information. For private help, contact the person who
provided your build. A dedicated verified contact and hosted support URL still
need to be supplied before App Store submission; no public mailbox is claimed.

## FAQ

**How do I use the bowl guide?**
Tap the floating bowl above navigation on any main screen. It offers shortcuts
for logging food, your grocery checklist and meals, plus a short, skippable tour.
The guide itself works locally and never sends an AI request. Hold the bowl to
move it to either side, replay the tour or hide it. Restore it in Profile under
Your companion. Adding food briefly triggers its celebration. AI chat is a
separate action and follows your account and sharing settings.

**Are discount notifications real?**
Mealio does not send retail promotion, stock or price-drop notifications from
its estimated-price catalog. Earlier catalog-based alerts are removed when the
updated app starts. No verified retailer promotion feed is connected.

**Are grocery prices live?**
No. Prices are estimates from a curated catalog, not verified promotions,
live store inventory or guaranteed shelf prices. You can enter a real price in
the shopping flow. Corrections apply only to the selected store. Older unscoped
corrections are preserved but need re-entry for a chosen store before they count
as your entered price. Check the retailer before purchasing.

**Why don't I see nearby stores?**
Make sure location access is enabled (Settings ▸ Mealio ▸ Location) and you have a
network connection. Without location, Mealio uses Vilnius centre.

**My meal plan didn't update after I changed my cart.**
You can tap **Regenerate** on the Meals tab. Online AI features require the
optional sharing permission, Sign in with Apple in Profile, available credits
and a working backend. Local suggestions
remain available when sharing is off or the service cannot be reached.

**How does the AI allowance work?**
Sign in with Apple in Profile to share one daily allowance across devices.
Signing in does not enable sharing by itself. The default allowance is 30 credits;
meal plans use 5 and short requests use 1. It resets at midnight UTC, shown in your
local time. Failed AI calls and cached responses do not spend personal credits.
Overall provider capacity can still cause temporary limits. Local features remain
available without sign-in or online AI. This pre-release service still requires
account-side activation; a disabled sign-in button does not mean your device is faulty.

**Are the calorie numbers exact?**
No — calorie and macro values are **estimates** and vary by product and
preparation. Mealio is not a medical device.

**How do I reset my data?**
Profile ▸ **Delete All Data** deletes your optional online AI account and clears
local profile, log, cart, budget and plans. If an AI account exists (even after
sign-out), reconnect and sign in again to finish deletion. The app reports a
failure rather than claiming deletion if the online step cannot complete.
A minimal keyed identifier and today's spent-credit count remain until the daily
reset to prevent allowance resets through account recreation.
If shopping sync was enabled, cloud deletion may remain pending until the app
reconnects with the original Apple Account. Health data on other devices stays
local to each device and must be deleted there.

**What does iCloud sync?**
Optional shopping sync covers cart, grocery list, budget and price corrections.
It does not sync body measurements, food logs, dietary restrictions or nutrition
plans. Use **Sync Now** or reopen the app to check for changes.

**Can I rely on suggestions for allergies?**
No. Preferences guide suggestions but cannot guarantee allergen safety. Check
every ingredient, product label and preparation method yourself.

**Does Mealio track me or sell my data?**
No. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
