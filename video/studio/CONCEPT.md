# Concept

**Logline:** In a painted magenta dusk, a sleepy bowl is woken by a falling light. One after another, a week of meals, a budget and a workout drop out of the sky into it, until the bowl is full, looks at us and becomes Nourlo.

**Why it matters:** the app's promise is that meals, money and training fit in one place. The bowl is that place, and it is the app's own character.

**Continuity device:** the bowl, centre frame, the whole time. Everything arrives along the vertical sky streaks.

## Beats (16 s)

| Time | What happens | Transition, camera and sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.9 | Dark dusk. A streak of light falls down the sky. | Whoosh. |
| 0.9 | The streak hits the bowl's rim. The world lights up. | Impact, bell, pad starts. Camera locked throughout. |
| 1.4–3.3 | The bowl opens its eyes. "Your whole week, in one bowl." | Blips, then a pluck. |
| 3.3–5.5 | A pile of fruit appears in the sky, the bowl looks up, it drops in. "Meals planned around you." | Shimmer, whoosh, landing pluck, beat starts. |
| 6.2–8.0 | Three coins hover, then fall one by one onto a stack. "Kept inside your budget." | Three clinks. |
| 9.0–10.6 | A dumbbell drops in with a heavy squash. "Workouts that fit your day." | Thud. |
| 11.0–12.4 | Breath: text clears, the full bowl looks at us and blinks. | Silence, a tick on the blink, a riser. |
| 12.5–16 | Payoff: light blooms from the bowl, it hops; "nourlo" paints on, then "Download now". Held for 2 s. | Hit, bell chord, a pluck per letter. |

**End card:** wordmark, "Meals · budget · training", "Download now" and "on the App Store".
