# Style bible (from ref.jpg)

**Palette**
- Sky magenta, light to dark: #D27BCB, #C468BB, #AE5FAD, #8E5193, #774E83
- Dark violet columns and edges: #5B4569, #4C3A5E
- Midground violet: #8A5A90, with a rim of #C87ABA
- Slate shadow side: #2F3F50 (subject), #23303C (deepest)
- Salmon lit side: #E8877A, with a peach edge of #F3A88F
- Mint rim on the far edge: #9BE0C0
- Grass: lavender scribbles #C79BC5 / #A27AA8 over green #3F5D50 / #2F4A42
- Cream for type and highlights: #FFF1E6

**Grounds**
- Sky: vertical dry-brush streaks, lighter in the centre, dark violet columns at the edges.
- Grass: lavender loops near the horizon, green upright strokes below.

**Shapes and line**
- Flat colour shapes, no gradients inside a shape.
- Every edge between two colours carries a comb of short bristle strokes (the dry-brush fringe). The slate shadow side bites into the salmon side with that fringe.
- Light comes from behind-left: salmon and peach on the lit side, mint on the far rim.

**Depth**
- Sky streaks, then violet hills, then the subject, then foreground grass overlapping its base.

**Type**
- Nunito Black in cream with a soft violet shadow for lines.
- Comfortaa Bold for the Nourlo wordmark.

**Motion**
- Locked camera.
- Things arrive vertically, in the direction of the sky streaks.
- Painted layers boil on 2s; each impact gives a squash, flying flecks and a ring.

**Sound**
- Soft pads.
- Plucks on landings, a metal clink for coins, a thud for weight.
- Silence for the breath.

**Do not copy**
- The warrior seen from behind, the sword, the hovering red robots, the soldiers, the huts and village.
- Only the look is taken.
